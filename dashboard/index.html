Handwritten Prescription OCR

Two approaches on the same data: a Tesseract OCR + fuzzy-match pipeline, and CNN classifiers (ResNet50, DenseNet121, ElasticNet CNN) over 78 medicine classes.

Headline results

Test split, 780 images, 78 classes (10 per class). Chance level is 1.3% accuracy and 0.50 AUC.

Pipeline

What the notebook does, end to end.

1Load dataDataset A: 4,680 labeled medicine crops. Dataset B: 129 full prescriptions.
2Detect ROIsAdaptive threshold, dilate, contours. Top 12 text blocks.
3Tesseract OCRBilateral filter, PSM 6, upscale small crops.
4Fuzzy matchrapidfuzz token-set ratio against 78 names, cutoff 75.
5Weak NERRegex for dosage and frequency, drug from lexicon.
6CNN classifiers3 models, 3 epochs, 224px, AUC / F1 on val and test.
